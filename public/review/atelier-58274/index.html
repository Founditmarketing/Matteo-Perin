01The Lookbook

The lookbook, edited.

The editorial view would open with a short, chosen story instead of giving all 78 photographs equal weight under a number.

Seventy-eight frames, one after another, each carrying only a number. Beautiful photographs presented like an inventory.

Proposed — composed from your frames

The statement.

A navy double-breasted dinner jacket with black satin peak lapels, closed over a white shirt and black bow tie, ending in burgundy tasselled loafers.

Corduroy, two ways.

One ribbed cloth cut two ways — a dark brown peak-lapel suit worn over an orange V-neck beside a burnt-orange double-breasted jacket falling to wide cargo-pocket trousers.

The close.

The same white leather jacket in two frames — open and mid-step, then buttoned square to the camera, the fringe hanging still along collar, placket and cuff.

All 78 frames in both books were viewed to choose these six.
