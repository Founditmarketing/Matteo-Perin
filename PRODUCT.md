# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

High-net-worth individuals and collectors: Jackson Hole residents and seasonal visitors who walk into the Deloney Ave showroom, plus a global online audience buying one-of-one Italian leather goods and commissioning bespoke pieces (up to a $185,000 crocodile jacket). They arrive through press, word of mouth, Google Ads, and local search. Context: browsing on phones in town or on large screens at home; they are fluent in luxury codes and immediately detect imitation. The job to be done: judge whether this house is the real thing, then buy a ready piece or start a commission conversation.

## Product Purpose

The site is the digital atelier of Matteo Perin, a bespoke Italian maker based in Jackson Hole, WY. It must (1) project world-class craft credibility on par with the houses it competes with, (2) sell the in-stock "Current Edit" of one-of-one pieces, and (3) convert qualified visitors into bespoke commission inquiries and deposits. Success: the site feels like the work of the same hands that make the garments; visitors inquire or purchase.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, assured, crafted. Quiet-luxury foundation (cream, charcoal, terracotta, Playfair Display serifs) but the owner wants it **bolder and more fashion-forward** than it currently reads: more editorial drama, more confident scale, more memorable composition. The energy of a runway house with the warmth of an Italian workshop. Emotions to evoke: awe at the craft, desire for the one-of-one, trust in the house.

## Anti-references

- Generic luxury e-commerce templates (Shopify-luxe sameness, interchangeable hero-grid-footer pages)
- Loud fashion-hype / streetwear energy (drops, countdowns, neon, all-caps shouting)
- Cold, sterile minimalism with no warmth (white-box galleries that feel like a hospital)
- Old-money clutter (ornate borders, busy serif walls, dated gold-on-black)

## Design Principles

1. **Editorial confidence** — compose pages like magazine spreads with deliberate asymmetry and pacing, never template grids.
2. **The garment is the hero** — photography and material texture lead; chrome recedes.
3. **Bold without noise** — drama comes from scale, contrast, and rhythm, not effects, gradients, or ornament.
4. **Craft in the details** — micro-typography, optical alignment, and restrained motion mirror the precision of tailoring.
5. **Scarcity made tangible** — one-of-one and by-commission must be felt in the design language (numbered pieces, ledger aesthetics), not just stated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain the existing prefers-reduced-motion handling and visible keyboard focus. Watch the known weak spot: low-contrast stone-gray text (#8C8C8C) on cream (#F2EFE9) used for small uppercase labels — AA requires rework or size/weight compensation. Touch targets on mobile galleries and dot indicators must stay ≥44px effective size.
